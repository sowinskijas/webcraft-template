FAQ

Questions we
always get asked

How long does a website take to build?

Most projects go from kickoff to live site in 1–4 weeks depending on scope. A simple 5-page site can be done in 7 days. Larger builds with custom features or e-commerce typically take 3–6 weeks. We give you a clear timeline before we start — no surprises.

Do I own my website after it's built?

Yes, 100%. You own every file, every image, every line of code. We hand everything over at launch. You're never locked into us — though most clients stick around for ongoing support because the relationship works.

Can you work with my existing booking or CRM software?

Absolutely. We integrate with Calendly, Acuity, HubSpot, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, and most other third-party platforms. If you use it, we can connect it to your site so your workflow stays exactly how you like it.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just a free 30-minute discovery call. We handle the strategy, copywriting, design, and build. If you have existing branding or content we'll use it — if not, we'll create it. The less you have to worry about, the better.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every site we build includes on-page SEO: proper meta tags, semantic HTML, fast load times, and Google Search Console setup. For Growth and Scale packages we also write keyword-optimized copy. Most clients see meaningful organic traffic within 60–90 days of launch.

What if I need changes after launch?

All packages include post-launch support (30–365 days depending on plan). After that, ongoing maintenance retainers are available. Simple text or image updates are quick and affordable — we're not going anywhere.
